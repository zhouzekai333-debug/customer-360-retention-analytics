# Job Search Targeting Framework — Stage II

**Updated:** 2026-09-10

This framework defines how target roles should be searched, screened and prioritised during Stage II. Job title alone is not sufficient. The aim is to maximise both near-term interview probability and long-term career capital while avoiding over-concentration in execution-heavy analytics roles that are increasingly exposed to AI automation.

## 1. Search scope

### Priority role families

**Tier A — primary long-term direction**
- Commercial Analyst
- Pricing Analyst
- Revenue Operations Analyst / Sales Operations Analyst
- Customer Insights Analyst / Customer Strategy Analyst
- CRM / Retention Analyst

**Tier B — selective applications**
- Product Analyst / Product Operations Analyst
- Business Performance Analyst
- Commercial Planning Analyst
- Marketing Analytics / Campaign Analytics roles with meaningful decision and stakeholder responsibility

**Tier C — entry-point only**
- Data Analyst
- BI / Reporting Analyst
- CRM Data Coordinator / Data Quality roles
- Reporting Officer / Dashboard-heavy analyst roles

Tier C roles remain valid if they create a strong first Australian analytics experience, but they should not become the intended 2–3 year destination.

## 2. Seniority and eligibility filter

Primary search range:
- Graduate
- Entry-level
- Junior
- 0–2 years experience

Stretch applications:
- 2–3 years when the evidence match is strong and the role does not contain a hard minimum that clearly excludes an early-career applicant.

Do not prioritise:
- 3+ years roles unless responsibilities and requirements are unusually accessible.
- Roles requiring clearly mandatory domain licences, citizenship/security clearance, or unrestricted permanent work rights that the candidate does not hold.

## 3. Geography and recency

Search locations:
- Sydney
- Melbourne
- Brisbane
- Gold Coast
- Adelaide
- Perth

Default posting window:
- Prefer roles posted within the last 5 days.
- Expand when the market is thin or a role is unusually high quality.

Avoid duplicate applications to previously submitted roles unless the employer has clearly reposted a materially different vacancy.

## 4. Three-score evaluation

Every serious role should be assessed on three separate dimensions.

### A. Match Score / 10

Measures the probability of getting an interview now.

Check:
- Required years of experience
- SQL / Excel / Power BI / Python / R requirements
- CRM / Salesforce / campaign / customer-data requirements
- Commercial/customer/marketing domain fit
- Stakeholder communication evidence
- Visa/work-right compatibility
- Location and availability
- Whether claims are supported by actual project/work evidence

Do not inflate project fit from the CV. For project-based claims, inspect the original project files/repository and use only evidence that is actually present.

### B. Career Capital Score / 10

Award up to 2 points for each:

1. **Commercial / revenue exposure** — pricing, margin, revenue, customer economics, forecasting or commercial performance.
2. **Stakeholder / business partnering** — regular work with Sales, Marketing, Finance, Product, Operations, IT or leadership.
3. **Project / process ownership** — ownership of a workstream, process improvement, implementation or cross-functional project.
4. **Decision responsibility** — recommendation, prioritisation, strategy, resource allocation or measurable business action.
5. **Domain depth** — customer, pricing, CRM, product, sales/revenue or commercial knowledge that compounds over time.

Interpretation:
- 8–10: high-priority career-building role
- 6–7: solid role with useful progression
- 4–5: mainly an entry point
- 0–3: weak long-term capital unless urgently needed for first experience

### C. AI Resilience

**Green — closer to the safe line**
- business partnering
- pricing/revenue decisions
- customer strategy
- cross-functional project work
- stakeholder influence
- prioritisation / recommendation responsibility
- process ownership

**Amber — transitional**
- meaningful analysis but mixed with routine execution
- campaign/marketing analytics with some recommendation responsibility
- product analytics with moderate stakeholder exposure
- general data analyst roles with ad-hoc business problem solving

**Red — execution-heavy**
- repeated data extraction
- dashboard refresh
- weekly/monthly reporting only
- record maintenance
- spreadsheet cleaning
- CRM administration without decision responsibility
- routine data-quality operations without broader ownership

AI Resilience does not mean that Green roles are immune to AI. It means that a larger share of value sits in organisational decision-making, judgement, stakeholder coordination and ownership rather than repeatable analytical production.

## 5. Application priority matrix

### Apply immediately
- Match >= 7/10 AND Career Capital >= 7/10
- No hard eligibility blocker
- Prefer Green or strong Amber AI Resilience

### Stretch / targeted application
- Match 5.5–6.5/10 but Career Capital >= 8/10
- 2–3 years experience requested but not clearly a hard exclusion
- Candidate has strong project/work evidence for the core problem

### Tactical entry role
- Match >= 8/10 but Career Capital 4–6/10
- Apply if it materially improves probability of obtaining the first formal Australian analytics role
- Treat as a 12–18 month stepping-stone, not the preferred long-term destination

### Deprioritise
- Match <5/10 with no distinctive advantage
- Career Capital <=3/10 unless an urgent income/career-entry need exists
- Role is mostly reporting/data maintenance and offers little stakeholder or decision exposure
- Hard eligibility blocker exists

## 6. JD reading order

For each job description, read in this order:

1. **Hard blockers** — work rights, citizenship/clearance, location, mandatory years/domain/licence.
2. **What business problem does the role own?**
3. **What decisions or actions follow from the analysis?**
4. **Who are the stakeholders?**
5. **What proportion of the role appears routine reporting/extraction?**
6. **Which technical skills are actually required?**
7. **Which existing project/work evidence proves the match?**
8. **What evidence gap would need CV tailoring or interview preparation?**

Do not start by counting keyword overlap.

## 7. Evidence selection rule

When tailoring applications:

- Inspect original project files before choosing a project.
- Select the project whose actual evidence best matches the JD.
- Never transfer a claim from one project to another because it sounds useful.
- Distinguish university, independent portfolio and professional experience clearly.
- Do not invent impact, stakeholders, tools or business outcomes.

Current project roles:
- **Customer 360 & Retention Analytics** — SQL, Power BI, data QA, RFM, lifecycle, CRM prioritisation, customer value.
- **Telecom Customer Analytics** — K-means, churn/customer-value trade-off, retention prioritisation and strategic recommendation.
- **Borgen campaign project** — multi-channel execution, communication, prioritisation and teamwork.

## 8. Funnel feedback loop

Track:

Application → Recruiter Screen → Hiring Manager / Technical → Final → Offer

Diagnose by stage:

- Low Application → Screen: targeting/CV/evidence/posting-age/channel problem.
- Good Screen → Hiring Manager but weak progression: interview comprehension, motivation, behavioural examples or fit.
- Technical failures: SQL recall, project defence, business insight or case structure.
- Final-round failures: stakeholder judgement, commercial depth, role fit, experience competition or practical constraints.

The next learning block should be based on the largest measured bottleneck rather than adding skills by intuition.

## 9. Career-direction rule

The long-term trajectory should move progressively from analytical execution toward business ownership:

Customer / Marketing Analytics
→ Customer / Commercial Analytics
→ Pricing / Revenue / CRM Strategy
→ Senior Commercial / Pricing / Revenue / Customer Strategy roles
→ Business partnering / strategy / management ownership

Technical capability remains required for interviews and credibility, but SQL/Power BI should increasingly support business decisions rather than define the whole role.
